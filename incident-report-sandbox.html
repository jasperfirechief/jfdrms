What This Sandbox Demonstrates

One evolving incident. No separate PCR, MVA, Fire, or NERIS report.
Patient-first when desired. Add Patient is available at the top on every incident.
Repeatable information. Patients, vitals, vehicles, occupants, apparatus, actions, hazards, casualties, and mutual aid can grow as needed.
Firefighter-friendly. Searchable standardized selections and checkboxes handle routine data while Other fields handle exceptions.
Offline-ready architecture. The production version can save the same evolving record locally and synchronize it later.
